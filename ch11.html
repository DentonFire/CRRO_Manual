    Chapter 11

    Special Programs and Future Initiatives

    11.1 Citizens Fire Academy (CFA)

    The Citizens Fire Academy is a multi-week educational program for community members. It did not run in FY 25-26, and no FY 26-27 date is set; do not promise one. The CRR Officer coordinates curriculum development, guest speakers, facilities, and participant logistics.

    11.2 Explorer Post 1291

    Fire Explorer Post 1291 serves youth ages 14 to 20 through Scouting America (formerly Scouting America) Exploring. Captain Kevin Tye is the post advisor. Instructor registration was renewed in Q1 FY 25-26 ($66.95 per instructor), and the 26/27 open house was held at Station 7 on August 24, 2026.

    • Renewal requires at least five registered Explorers.
    • Every adult instructor needs current registration and safety training.
    • Route interested youth to joinexploring.org, then select Post 1291. In replies, give the link and ask whether they have filled out the application yet; copy Captain Tye when a family needs the next meeting date.
    • Homeschool students apply the same way as everyone else.
    • [ACTION REQUIRED] The City Programs Offered page says ages 14 to 19 and Denton County residents; this manual says 14 to 20. Confirm age and residency with Captain Tye before promising out-of-county families a spot.

    11.3 Clown Program

    Fire prevention clown program was inactive in Q1 FY 25-26. Requires identifying and training performers if the program resumes. This is a succession planning need.

    11.4 Fire Camp

    Annual youth fire camp program. Status: TBD for FY 25-26. Requires coordination for purchasing, staffing, meals, school permissions, and partner involvement.

    11.5 Smoke Detector Program

    Community smoke detector installation program. In Q1 FY 25-26, 50 smoke detectors were installed in community residences. Track installations through ESO for quarterly reporting.

    11.6 Hoarding Awareness Campaign

    Video-based community education campaign on hoarding dangers. Campaign launched January-February 2026 with MarComm video content production. Track engagement metrics through social media analytics.

    11.7 DFR/UAS Program

    The CRR Officer serves as UAS Program Manager (FD side) for the Joint Public Safety UAS Team operated with Denton PD. On the PD side, DC Cose is UAS Program Director and Acting DC Briggs is UAS Program Manager.

    11.8 Succession Planning Needs

    Several programs require succession planning beyond the CRR Officer position. These are documented in Chapter 17 (System Administration & Succession Planning).
