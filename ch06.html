    6.3 Newsletter Production Workflow

    1. Topic selection: Review incident data and seasonal hazard calendar. Identify the primary risk topic for the month.
    2. Content development: Research the topic, gather statistics (national and local), develop 3-5 actionable safety tips.
    3. Draft in Constant Contact: Follow the standard template structure (see below). Include DFD branding and images.
    4. Review: Proofread for accuracy, check all links, verify statistics.
    5. Send: Schedule or send the newsletter. Typical send day: first week of the month.
    6. Track analytics: After 48 hours, review open rates, click-through rates, and engagement.
    7. Archive link: Save the Constant Contact archive URL for quarterly report appendix.

    Standard Newsletter Structure

    • Opening hook: Seasonal or timely connection to grab reader attention
    • Main safety topic: 3-5 specific, actionable recommendations
    • Supporting statistics: National data to contextualize the risk
    • DFD resources mention: Link to relevant tools, programs, or services
    • Call to action: Share the newsletter, contact DFD, attend an event, or access a resource

    6.4.1 Overview

    The Ready to Serve newsletter follows a 12-month editorial calendar aligned with the Civil Service hiring cycle. The cycle begins in February with a comprehensive career overview and progresses through each phase of the hiring process, culminating with exam preparation content in November and December before the January Civil Service Exam.

    Each month's newsletter is supported by four weekly social media posts across Facebook, LinkedIn, and X (Twitter). These posts reinforce the newsletter theme while adapting tone and format for each platform's audience and conventions.

    6.4.3 Publishing Schedule

    Newsletter: First Tuesday of each month

    Facebook: Every Monday

    LinkedIn: Every Wednesday

    X (Twitter): Every Friday

    This schedule ensures consistent weekly touchpoints while spacing content across the week. The newsletter establishes the month's theme, and social posts reinforce that theme throughout the month.

    6.4.4 Platform Guidelines

    Facebook

    Tone: Conversational and direct. Speak to candidates as individuals. Format: 2-4 sentences, end with a clear call to action when appropriate. Use complete sentences and accessible language. Avoid jargon.

    LinkedIn

    Tone: Professional but not corporate. Speak to career-minded candidates. Format: 2-3 sentences with polished phrasing. Reference career development, professional growth, and institutional values.

    X (Twitter)

    Tone: Punchy and direct. Every word earns its place. Format: Under 280 characters. Short declarative sentences. No hashtags unless campaign-specific. End with impact, not trailing thoughts.

    6.4.7 Implementation Notes

    1. Update all date references (registration window, exam date, PAT and offer timing) at the beginning of each fiscal year. Denton does not run its own recruit academy; do not publish an academy start date.
    2. The January edition content should be finalized in December and scheduled in advance to ensure timely delivery before the exam.
    3. Social media posts can be scheduled using Constant Contact's social media scheduler or a third-party tool such as Buffer or Hootsuite.
    4. Monitor engagement metrics monthly. If a particular topic or format consistently outperforms others, consider adjusting the calendar accordingly.
    5. Cross-promote content: link newsletter signup in social posts, and include social follow buttons in newsletters.
    6. All content should align with DFD branding standards: professional tone, no em dashes, no AI writing tells, active voice, and concrete language.

    6.5 Constant Contact Administration

    The CRR Officer is the sole administrator of the DFD Constant Contact account. Key administrative tasks include:

    • Managing subscriber lists (separate lists for CRR and Recruiting audiences)
    • Creating and scheduling email campaigns
    • Monitoring deliverability, bounce rates, and spam complaints
    • Maintaining compliance with CAN-SPAM requirements (unsubscribe functionality, physical address, sender identification)
    • Exporting analytics data for quarterly reports
    • Archiving newsletter links for appendices (archive URLs follow format: conta.cc/XXXXXXX)

    Constant Contact HTML Rules

    When pasting custom HTML into Constant Contact:

    • Do not include Open Graph meta tags (property="og:...").
    • Remove the [[trackingImage]] merge tag; tracking is automatic now.
    • Keep standard head meta only. A hidden preheader div for inbox preview text is fine, as are inline styles and style-block media queries.
    • In VerifEYE emails, the test link goes after the sign-off, never inline in the body.
